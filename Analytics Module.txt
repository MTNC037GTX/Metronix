1. Financial Overview Dashboard + cashflow analytics
This is the executive summary.

Metrics:
Total income
Total expense
Net cash flow = income - expense
Savings rate = (income - expense) / income × 100
Average daily spending
Cash runway = cash balance / avg monthly expense

How to show:
KPI cards for all metrics
Net cash flow trend = mini line chart
Savings rate = progress bar with threshold markers

2. Income Analytics
Purpose: income stability and growth tracking.

Metrics:
Total income per period
Income by source
Income growth rate = (current - previous) / previous × 100
Income volatility = std deviation / mean

How to show:
Line chart for income trend
Horizontal bar chart for income by source (ranked)
Small KPI badge for growth rate
Text indicator for volatility (low / medium / high)

Insights:
Best performing income source
Income dependency risk (single source > 70%)

3. Expense Analytics
This is where users actually change behavior.

Metrics:
Total expense
Expense by category
Fixed vs variable expense ratio
Top 5 expense categories
Expense growth rate
Expense anomaly detection

How to show:
Line chart for total expense trend
Horizontal bar chart for expense by category
Stacked bar chart for fixed vs variable
Ranked list for top 5 categories
Dot marker on line chart for anomaly months


4. CATEGORY-LEVEL ANALYTICS

Metrics:
Category monthly spending
Category budget utilization = actual / budget × 100
Category trend slope

How to show:
Per-category mini line chart
Progress bar for budget utilization

Color coding:
< 80% green
80% to 100% yellow
100% red

5. Budget Performance Analytics
This connects planning to reality.

Metrics:
Total budget vs actual
Overspend amount
Underspend amount
Chronic overspend flag

How to show:
Grouped bar chart (budget vs actual)
Heatmap table by category and month
Warning badge for chronic overspend

Views:
Budget vs actual per category
Heat indicator:
< 80% = green
80% to 100% = yellow
100% = red

Insight:
Chronic overspend categories (3 consecutive months)

6. Asset and Net Worth Analytics

Metrics:

Net worth change
Net worth growth rate

How to show:

Delta indicator for growth rate

Views:
Asset class breakdown
Net worth monthly change
